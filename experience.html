Apna Thikana

More than just a cafe—it's the heartbeat of Sirsia, Giridih.

Every great city has that one spot. The place where plans are made, where laughter rings loudest over a steaming cup of tea, and where the food feels like a warm hug. For Giridih, that spot didn't exist in the way we wanted it to. We wanted the premium, polished vibe of a metro city, but we didn't want to lose the desi soul and chatpata flavors of Jharkhand.

That's how The Nukkad Cafe was born on Block Road, Kalika Kunj Colony.

We didn't just open a restaurant; we built an "Apna Thikana"—your personal corner. Whether you're a student looking for a quiet place to code, friends meeting up after college, or family craving our famous Awadhi Dum Biryani on a Sunday evening, this space was designed for you.

We pair premium, moody aesthetics with food that hits right at home. From our expertly crafted Momos to the simple, undeniable comfort of a Kulhad Chai, we are here to serve memories, one plate at a time.

Local Soul, Premium Vibe

We believe you shouldn't have to leave Giridih to experience a top-tier cafe atmosphere. We brought the modern glassmorphism aesthetic right to Sirsia.

Uncompromising Taste

From the fiery kick of our Dehati Chicken to the perfect crisp of our French Fries, our kitchen uses fresh ingredients to ensure every bite is bold and authentic.

Frictionless Experience

We embrace technology to make your life easier. No clunky apps or confusing payment gateways—just a smooth, direct WhatsApp ordering system built for the way you actually communicate.

Be Part of the Story

Drop by Block Road and say hello, or let us bring the Nukkad experience straight to your living room.
