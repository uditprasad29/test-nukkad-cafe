Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to The Nukkad Cafe. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website and use our online ordering system.

1. Information We Collect

To process your delivery orders, we collect the following personal information directly provided by you during the checkout process:

  • Full Name
  • Phone Number
  • Delivery Address

2. How We Store Your Data (Local Storage)

For your convenience, your Name, Phone Number, and Delivery Address are saved locally on your own device's browser using "Local Storage." This ensures you do not have to re-enter your details every time you visit our website. We do not store this data on any external servers or databases.

3. How We Process Orders (WhatsApp)

When you click "Place Order," your cart details and personal information are formatted into a text message and securely transferred to your personal WhatsApp application. The order is officially placed only when you send this message to our official cafe number (+91 96610 23090).

4. Data Sharing and Protection

We strictly use your information solely for the purpose of preparing and delivering your food. We will never sell, rent, or distribute your personal information to any third-party marketing companies or external entities.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at our physical location at Block Road, Kalika Kunj Colony, Sirsia, Giridih, or via WhatsApp at +91 96610 23090.
