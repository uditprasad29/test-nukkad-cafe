Terms of Service

Rules and regulations for ordering from The Nukkad Cafe.

By accessing this website and placing an order with The Nukkad Cafe, you accept and agree to be bound by the terms and provision of this agreement.

1. Ordering Mechanism

All online orders generated through this website are transmitted via WhatsApp. An order is not confirmed until it has been sent via WhatsApp and confirmed by a representative of The Nukkad Cafe.

2. Return, Replacement, and Cancellation Policy

Because we deal in freshly prepared, perishable food items, our cancellation and return policies are strictly enforced to maintain quality and prevent food wastage.

STRICT NO RETURN POLICY: Food cannot be returned or replaced once the order is placed and confirmed by our kitchen.

Please verify your cart items, quantities, and delivery address carefully before confirming your order via WhatsApp. Once preparation has begun, the order cannot be modified, canceled, returned, or replaced.

3. Pricing and Payments

All prices listed on the website are in Indian Rupees (₹). While we strive for accuracy, prices are subject to change without prior notice. Payment methods (Cash on Delivery, UPI, etc.) will be finalized during the WhatsApp confirmation process.

4. Delivery Radius and Times

We serve specific locations within Giridih, Jharkhand. We reserve the right to decline orders that fall outside our serviceable area or during extreme weather conditions to ensure the safety of our delivery partners.

5. Contact Information

For any disputes or inquiries, you may visit us at Block Road, Kalika Kunj Colony, Sirsia, Giridih, or message us on our official WhatsApp number: +91 96610 23090.
